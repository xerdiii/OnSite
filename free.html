About the address

Your free page goes live on an address we give you — something like your-business.sitehouse.eu — on our hosting, with the padlock already on it. You do not buy a domain, touch DNS, or set anything up. That is the part that stops most people, so we took it off you entirely. When you want your own name instead — yourbusiness.com — we register it and move the page across for €10 a month, and the old address keeps working.
