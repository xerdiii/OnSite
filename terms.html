01

Overview & service scope

Xovah (“we”, “the Provider”) agrees to design, host and maintain your business website according to the products and services you select at checkout. You (“you”, “the customer”) agree to the terms below when you place that order.

Depending on what you select, your website may be a standard landing page or a custom multi-section business website. Hosting, SSL/HTTPS and the technical maintenance included in your selection are provided for as long as the relevant part of your order is active and paid for.

02

Website features & scope

You choose your website features at checkout, and what you choose there is what we build.

  • The Custom Website (€50, one-time) requires a minimum of three selected features. Selecting more than three does not change the price.
  • The Professional Website (€200, one-time) includes all ten website features as standard, with nothing to select. It does not include any monthly service — those are chosen and billed separately.
  • Any other feature or service is included only if it was specifically selected and paid for in your order.

Revisions. Getting the website to match what you selected and the content you supplied is part of the build, not an extra. During your review we correct anything that is wrong, missing or misread. What is not included at that stage is changing your mind about the direction — a different layout, a different structure or features you did not order. Those are quoted separately, and we tell you the price before doing anything.

Work outside your selected package is not included. Large redesigns, custom software, advanced functionality, e-commerce systems, additional pages beyond the agreed scope and similar work require a separate quote and a separate agreement before we start. We do not offer unlimited custom work at these prices.

03

Website maintenance

If you subscribe to Website Maintenance (€19.99 / month), it covers up to 15 reasonable website changes per month. A reasonable change is a small content update, for example:

  • Changing text on the page
  • Replacing photos
  • Updating opening hours
  • Updating prices
  • Updating business information
  • Other small content changes of a similar size

Unused changes do not roll over into the next month. Major redesigns, new functionality, new pages beyond the agreed scope, custom development and large structural changes are not included and may require an additional quote.

04

Payment

One-time project charges are split 25/75.

  • 25% deposit — paid before development begins. This is what starts the project.
  • Remaining 75% — becomes due once the website is completed and presented to you for approval. We do not take it automatically before you have reviewed the finished site.

Once the remaining 75% is paid, the website goes live or is transferred according to the service you selected, any monthly services begin, and your dashboard access opens.

Monthly subscriptions are separate from that. They begin when your website goes live — you are not charged for a monthly service while the site is still being built.

Every one-time and recurring charge is shown in the order summary before you pay, split into what is due today, what is due on approval, and what recurs monthly after launch.

05

Delivery

Our standard target is to deliver your website within 7 days of receiving all the content and information we need from you. This is a target we work to, not an unconditional guarantee.

The 7 days start only once everything has reached us. If content, approvals, feedback, login details, images or business information arrive late or incomplete, the delivery date moves out accordingly. We will tell you what is still outstanding.

06

Monthly billing

Monthly services are billed automatically each month according to what you selected. Your monthly amount depends entirely on which services are active:

  • Online Booking System — €29.99 / month
  • Website Maintenance — €19.99 / month
  • Review Management — €29.99 / month
  • Digital Menu — €19.99 / month, after the one-time €49.99 setup fee

Billing starts when your website goes live and continues each month until you cancel, under the cancellation terms below.

07

Failed payments

If a monthly payment fails, we will:

  • Notify you that the payment did not go through

  • Require any outstanding balance to be settled before reactivation

We do not delete your data because a payment failed. Suspension is a pause, not a deletion.

Cancellation & refunds

Monthly services run month to month unless agreed otherwise, and you can cancel them. Once cancelled, the service stops at the end of the period you have already paid for.

Payments already processed for the current billing period are generally non-refundable, unless applicable law requires otherwise or we have specifically agreed otherwise in writing.

The one-time deposit pays for starting your project. Once work has begun it is generally not refundable, subject to your rights under applicable consumer law. Nothing here removes a right you have that cannot legally be excluded.

How to cancel. Monthly services: turn them off under My Services in your dashboard, or email us — both work, and neither requires a reason. A project: email us and say you want to stop. Cancellation of a monthly service takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for.

Statutory withdrawal rights. Where consumer or distance-selling rules give you a right to withdraw, that right applies regardless of what this agreement says. Where those rules apply you have fourteen days to withdraw. Because a website is built to order, paying the deposit is a request for us to begin during that period: you keep the right to withdraw, but if you do so after work has started you are charged for what was already done, and the right ends once the site is finished and delivered. If you would rather we did not start until the fourteen days are up, say so and we will wait.

The full detail is in the Cancellation & Refund Policy.

Where it is registered in ours as part of a managed service, it stays with us when the service ends — and in that case you can ask us to transfer it to you, subject to registrar transfer rules, any applicable transfer or renewal costs, and your account being settled.

We will not pretend otherwise about what is public: anything served to a browser, including the frontend markup, styles and scripts of your live site, can be viewed and copied by anyone who visits it. That is how the web works. What we control is the official project and the infrastructure that runs it, not the visibility of published pages.

If you want to leave and take an export or the source files with you, ask us. Where you have purchased an explicit source-code transfer or ownership option, we will hand over the agreed files once the applicable payment and requirements are complete.
