Why a quarter, and not the whole thing?

Because you have never met us. Handing a stranger four hundred euro for a website that doesn't exist yet is a bad deal, and we'd rather not ask for it.

So you pay a quarter, we build the thing, and you look at it on your own phone before the rest is due. If it's wrong, we fix it. If you walk away, you've spent a quarter, not the lot.

The other half of it: we get paid for finishing, not for starting. That tends to focus the mind.

There's no discovery call, no strategy workshop and no onboarding sequence. You send us your opening hours and some photos. We send you back a website.

Straight answers

One-time, monthly, and what happens next.

The eight that come up most. The rest are answered in the full FAQ.

What does the €79.99 cover?
The custom website itself, built with the features you select — that is the base website price, paid one time. Choosing more than three features does not increase it.
What is the difference between €79.99 and €249.99?
The €79.99 Custom Website is built around the features you choose, with three as the minimum. The €249.99 Professional Website is the complete build: every feature on this page is included as standard, so there is nothing to select. Both are paid one time, and both take the same optional add-ons.
Which services repeat every month?
Online Booking System (€29.99 / month), Website Maintenance (€19.99 / month), Review Management (€29.99 / month), and the Digital Menu monthly fee (€19.99 / month). Everything else on this page is paid once.
Are Business Extras required?
No. Buy the website on its own if that's all you want. Most people do.
Are Business Extras monthly payments?
No — they're one-time, every one of them. Nothing there quietly becomes a monthly bill.
How much do I actually pay today?
A quarter of the one-time total. That's it for now. The rest is due once the site is built and you've said you're happy with it, and nothing monthly gets charged until you're actually live. The order summary spells out all three numbers before you pay anything.
How long does it take?
About a week from the day we have everything — logo, photos, your services and prices, opening hours, how people reach you. It's a target, not a promise carved in stone: if the photos land three weeks later, so does the website.
Can I add or stop a monthly service later?
Yes. Monthly services are separate from the website. You can start with none of them, add one when you need it, and stop it without affecting the website you already paid for.
