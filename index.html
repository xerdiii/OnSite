Straight answers

What you pay, and what happens next.

The ones that come up most. The rest are answered in the full FAQ.

What does the €50 cover?
The custom website itself, built with the sections you select from the 34 — that is the base website price, paid one time. Choosing more of them does not increase it.
What is the difference between the three?
The €50 Custom Website builds any twenty of the 34, your choice, with three as the minimum. The €200 Full Website builds all thirty-four with no cap, and does the setup work every site needs anyway — search indexing, analytics, the cookie banner and legal pages, spam protection. The €500 Complete Package is the Full Website plus the work around it: maintenance, Local SEO, a logo and menu design, WhatsApp Business and business email, and three years of hosting instead of one. All three are paid one time.
What happens to my domain and hosting?
Your domain, the hosting and the security padlock are included — the first year with Custom and Professional, the first three years with Complete. You pick the domain name yourself: type the one you want before you order and we register it in your name.
Are Business Extras required?
No. All 34 sections and features come with every website. The add-ons are extra work on top, and buying none of them is a perfectly normal order.
How much do I actually pay today?
A quarter of the one-time total. That is it for now. The rest is due once the site is built and you have said you are happy with it. The order summary spells out all three numbers before you pay anything.
How long does it take?
About a week from the day we have everything — logo, photos, your services and prices, opening hours, how people reach you. It is a target, not a promise carved in stone: if the photos land three weeks later, so does the website.

Why a quarter, and not the whole thing?

Because you have never met us. Handing a stranger hundreds of euro for a website that does not exist yet is a bad deal, and we would rather not ask for it.

So you pay a quarter, we build the thing, and you look at it on your own phone before the rest is due. If it is wrong, we fix it. If you walk away, you have spent a quarter, not the lot.

The other half of it: we get paid for finishing, not for starting. That tends to focus the mind.

No discovery call, no strategy workshop, no onboarding sequence. You send us your opening hours and some photos. We send you back a website.
