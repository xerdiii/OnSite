Straight answers

€35 / month, for as long as you want the website. That is the whole price — nothing before it and nothing beside it.

No. Both are included and managed by us. You never open a hosting account, you never log into a domain panel, and you never get a separate bill for either.

The honest answer: hosting and domain management stop, and your website may be taken offline. The subscription is what keeps it running.

No minimum term. It is a monthly subscription: it continues while you pay for it, and it ends when you stop.

Yes. Technical maintenance and updates are part of the subscription. New hours, a new service, a new photo — send it over and we handle it.
