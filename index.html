Straight answers

One-time, monthly, and what happens next.

The ones that come up most. The rest are answered in the full FAQ.

What does the €79.99 cover?
The custom website itself, built with the sections you select from the 34 — that is the base website price, paid one time. Choosing more of them does not increase it.
What is the difference between the three?
The €79.99 Custom Website is built around the sections you choose from the 34, with three as the minimum. The €249.99 Professional Website has all 34 built in, so there is nothing to select. The €900 Complete Package is that same website plus the work around it — maintenance, Local SEO, the online menu, a logo and menu design, WhatsApp Business and business email — and three years of hosting instead of one. All three are paid one time.
What happens to my domain and hosting?
Your domain, the hosting and the security padlock are included — the first year with Custom and Professional, the first three years with Complete. After that it is €10 a month, and that is the only thing that changes. You pick the domain name yourself: type the one you want before you order and we register it in your name.
Which services repeat every month?
The ten monthly services — maintenance, content updates, image and text changes, SEO upkeep and reporting, Google Business Profile upkeep, performance monitoring, security and backup monitoring, priority support — from €10 to €40 a month each. Plus the €10 a month for domain and hosting once your included years run out. Everything else on this page is paid once.
Are Business Extras required?
No. All 34 sections and features come with every website. The add-ons are extra work on top, and buying none of them is a perfectly normal order.
Are Business Extras monthly payments?
No — they are one-time, every one of them. Nothing there quietly becomes a monthly bill.
How much do I actually pay today?
A quarter of the one-time total. That is it for now. The rest is due once the site is built and you have said you are happy with it, and nothing monthly gets charged until you are actually live. The order summary spells out all three numbers before you pay anything.
How long does it take?
About a week from the day we have everything — logo, photos, your services and prices, opening hours, how people reach you. It is a target, not a promise carved in stone: if the photos land three weeks later, so does the website.
Can I add or stop a monthly service later?
Yes. Monthly services are separate from the website. You can start with none of them, add one when you need it, and stop it without affecting the website you already paid for.

Why a quarter, and not the whole thing?

Because you have never met us. Handing a stranger hundreds of euro for a website that does not exist yet is a bad deal, and we would rather not ask for it.

So you pay a quarter, we build the thing, and you look at it on your own phone before the rest is due. If it is wrong, we fix it. If you walk away, you have spent a quarter, not the lot.

The other half of it: we get paid for finishing, not for starting. That tends to focus the mind.

No discovery call, no strategy workshop, no onboarding sequence. You send us your opening hours and some photos. We send you back a website.
