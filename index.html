      Straight answers

      One-time, monthly, and what happens next.

      What does the €79.99 cover?
      The custom website itself, built with the features you select — that is the base website price, paid one time. Choosing more than three features does not increase it.
      What is the difference between €79.99 and €249.99?
      The €79.99 Custom Website is built around the features you choose, with three as the minimum. The €249.99 Professional Website is the complete build: every feature on this page is included as standard, so there is nothing to select. Both are paid one time, and both take the same optional add-ons.
      Why at least three features?
      A page with fewer than three does not give a visitor enough to act on. Three is the minimum that makes the custom website worth building for you. The Professional Website has no minimum, because everything is already in it.
      Is Local SEO a subscription?
      No. Local SEO is €199.99 paid one time, as an upgrade to your website. It does not renew and it does not appear on a monthly bill.
      Which services repeat every month?
      Online Booking System (€29.99 / month), Website Maintenance (€19.99 / month), Review Management (€29.99 / month), and the Digital Menu monthly fee (€19.99 / month). Everything else on this page is paid once.
      What counts as a website change?
      Anything you ask us to update on the site — a price, a service, a photo, your hours, a phone number. Website Maintenance includes up to 15 changes per month.
      How does Review Management work?
      We help you manage the reviews on your Google Business profile and Google Maps — watching for new ones and helping you reply — and send review notifications to your email where that is technically possible.
      Why does the Digital Menu have two prices?
      €49.99 is a one-time setup fee for building the menu. €19.99 / month keeps it running and lets it be updated. Both are shown separately in your order so they are never confused.
      How much do I actually pay today?
      Half of your one-time total, as a deposit to start the project. The other half is due once the website is finished and you have approved it. Monthly services are not billed at all until your site is live — the order summary shows all three numbers before you pay.
      How long does it take?
      Within 7 days of receiving everything we need from you — logo, photos, business information, services and prices, opening hours, contact details, social links and any text you want used. That is a target we work to, not an unconditional guarantee: if content arrives late, the delivery date moves with it.
      Can I add or stop a monthly service later?
      Yes. Monthly services are separate from the website. You can start with none of them, add one when you need it, and stop it without affecting the website you already paid for.
