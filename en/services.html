How extras are paid for

Extras join your one-time total and are paid once, when your site is ready to go live. There is no subscription on any of them and nothing renews by itself.

→ Where a price starts with “from”, the job varies enough that a single figure would be a guess. We confirm the real number with you in writing before anything is charged.
